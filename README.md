# Mad Libs Generator

A simple interactive command-line Mad Libs game written in Python. Users can choose from three story templates and fill in parts of speech to generate funny stories.

## Story Templates

1. **Hospital Visit** – A story about a funny experience at a hospital.
2. **Camping Trip** – An outdoor adventure story featuring camping, wildlife, and campfires.
3. **Enchanted Castle** – A letter written from a magical castle in a faraway land.

## Requirements

* Python 3.x (no external libraries or dependencies required)
